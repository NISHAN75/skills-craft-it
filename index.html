Why Skills Craft IT?

One Connected Platform for Smarter
Transport & Business Operations

From live fleet visibility to back-office reporting, every module runs on one system built for how your business actually operates.

Trip Record

Keep every trip organized in one place. Search trip history, manage records, generate invoices, and maintain complete operational visibility.

Booking Agent Panel

Give booking agents a dedicated workspace to create bookings, manage trip information, and monitor trip progress in real time.

Monthly Billing Automation

Generate hundreds or thousands of trip bills in minutes. Reduce manual work, billing errors, and month-end pressure through automation.

Standard Accounting

Combine traditional deposit-expense tracking with structured accounting practices for better financial control, reporting, and transparency.

Expense & Maintenance Log

Record operational expenses, maintenance activities, supporting documents, and screenshots to improve accountability and reduce unnecessary costs.

Fleet Management

Manage vehicle records, maintenance history, fuel cost, toolbox records, operational status, and fleet-related information from one centralized platform.

GPS Integration

Connect GPS tracking directly with your management software so operations and live vehicle information stay within one connected environment.

Fuel Cost Control

Track every litre of fuel, mileage, refueling records, and trip-wise fuel expenses to identify leakage, control costs, and improve fleet efficiency.

Document Expiry Reminder

Receive timely reminders for fitness certificates, tax tokens, insurance, route permits, licenses, and other important documents—helping reduce expiry-related risks and penalties.

Staff Salary Management

Manage driver and staff salaries, advances, deductions, allowances, and payment history through a clear and centralized record system.

Custom Software Development

Your business does not have to adjust to rigid software. Skills Craft IT can customize features and workflows around your logistics and operational requirements.
